# Grid Souls: the story (approved)

## The soul and the truth (revealed a memory at a time)

You were **Rowan Thale**, bell-keeper of Saltmere, a fishing town on a cliff.
The great bell in the tower was the town's only storm warning: when it rang,
the boats came home.

Rowan never wanted the bell. The town gave it to Rowan because Rowan's
younger sibling, **Mira**, was too young, and everyone loved Mira more.
Rowan rang it, mostly, and put everything else off.

On the night of the great storm, the bell stayed silent. Rowan had promised
to mend its frayed rope and never did; had spent the evening at the harvest
feast; had taken a merchant's silver to keep a rotten ship at sea; and was
waiting, drunk and bitter, for a boat carrying **Iven**, who had left Rowan
for another life. Mira climbed the tower to ring the bell. They fought on
the stair. Rowan struck out, the old rope snapped, and both of them fell.
Eleven boats did not come home. Iven's was one of them.

Rowan has told themself ever since that it was the storm.

The trial is the bell tower turned inside out: seven floors, each a sin,
each smaller than the last, until there's nowhere left to stand but the truth.

## The seven memories (one returned by each boss)

| Floor | Sin | Boss | Memory |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sloth | The Unfinished | **A broken promise**: "Tomorrow," Rowan told Mira, about the frayed rope. Every day, tomorrow. |
| 2 | Gluttony | The Feast | **The harvest feast**: the clouds came in over the sea while Rowan drank and laughed and didn't look up. |
| 3 | Greed | The Collector | **Silver**: a merchant's purse, and a ship Rowan knew was rotten kept at sea. |
| 4 | Envy | The Favoured (Mira, with your own moves) | **The favourite**: everyone's smile turned to Mira when she walked in. Rowan kept count. |
| 5 | Obsession | The Beloved (Iven) | **Iven**: a boat leaving the harbour, and Rowan watching the horizon every night after. |
| 6 | Wrath | The Storm on the Stair | **The stair**: Mira's hand on the rope, Rowan's hand on Mira. The rope snapping. |
| 7 | Pride | Rowan | **The full truth**: "It was the storm," says the soul across the last three tiles. It wasn't. |

## Floor 1 in full

**Opening** (white text on black, before the first floor):
> You wake on cold stone, above nothing.
> You do not know your name.
> Somewhere far below, a bell is ringing.
> It should have rung long ago.

**The Unfinished** (boss intro: the camera sweeps the floor, ash rises into
a shape, its name is carved into the slabs):
> A hollow giant of half-made things: a rope spliced and never finished, a
> letter never sent, a bell with a crack running through it. It moves the way
> chores wait: slowly, and it never goes away.

Its name is carved as: **THE UNFINISHED**.

**Memory I: A broken promise** (text over a still glyph picture of a bell
tower in the rain):
> The rope was fraying. Mira showed me, twice.
> "I'll mend it tomorrow," I said.
> I said it in spring. I said it at harvest.
> I said it the night the clouds came in over the sea.
> Tomorrow never once came.

**With guilt** (one more line if you died 5 times or more on this floor):
> I am still saying it.

## Guilt and endings

- **Guilt** (deaths) only changes the words: extra lines in the memories,
  and which ending is offered.
- **Accept**: Rowan admits it and the bell finally rings: the soul passes on.
- **Refuse**: Rowan insists it was the storm and becomes a bell-ringer on
  Sloth's floor.
- **Hidden**: lay down the sword when a boss is broken instead of
  finishing it (each boss "forgiven" adds to it); with all seven forgiven,
  Mira is waiting at the bottom of the tower.

## The game's bell-ringers

The bell-ringer enemies on floor 1 echo Rowan's job, and the eye in the sky
is the town watching.
